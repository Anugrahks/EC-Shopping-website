How ordering works

Add the products and quantities you need to your cart, provide your name, phone number, and delivery address at checkout, and choose cash on delivery. Delivery is intended for Panniyur, Kannur and surrounding areas; confirm availability and timing with the shop. Online order notifications may not always be available, so if checkout says an order was only saved on your device, contact the shop to confirm it was received before expecting delivery.
